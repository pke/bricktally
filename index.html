Verify Your LEGO Sets Are Complete

BrickTally helps you count every piece in your LEGO® sets before selling, storing, or building. Enter a set number to get a full parts checklist powered by the Rebrickable database.

  • Track parts, spare parts, and minifigures separately
  • Filter by colour to focus on one group at a time
  • Export missing parts to BrickLink, Pick a Brick, Rebrickable, or as a plain text list
  • Generate a completion badge for eBay or marketplace listings
  • Optimised for tablets and phones — always at your fingertips when sorting pieces
  • Works offline as a PWA — install it on your device
  • Backup and restore your progress across devices
